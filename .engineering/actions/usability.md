# Action: usability
Intent/scope: Run one usability round per workflow/usability.md on the given screens or flow: personas, tasks, two isolated lay-user instances on the lightest tier, triage, and the prepared decision brainstorm. Without arguments propose the next round from the board and the previous reports.
Responsible specialist: ux-researcher; principal dispatches the two lay users, persists their reports verbatim, convenes the brainstorm and records decisions.
Input: screens/pages or build reference, audience description, business-rules registry, privacy findings, previous reports and decisions, viewports/themes in scope.
Output: persona files, per-persona reports, triage table, decision records, board issues for fixes, and the round summary. Evidence class is "simulated lay users"; state it.
Lay users receive only persona, task and permitted screens. Decisions follow the precedence: user safety and privacy law > crucial business rules > usability > copy > preference. Items needing legal judgment are marked [LEGAL-REVIEW] for the human. Common policies and workflow apply to standalone invocations too.
